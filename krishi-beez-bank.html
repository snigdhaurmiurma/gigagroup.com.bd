Products & Capabilities

Enterprise Solutions & Offerings

Comprehensive services and technical solutions engineered by Krishi Beez Bank.

🌱

High-Yield Crop Seeds

Supplying certified Boro and Aman paddy, hybrid maize, high-protein wheat, and export-grade seasonal vegetable seeds with maximum vitality.

❄️

Climate-Controlled Seed Banking

Humidity and temperature-regulated germplasm facilities ensuring zero genetic degradation, high vigour, and long-term viability.

🌦️

Climate-Resilient Varieties

Submergence-tolerant and saline-tolerant seed strains engineered in partnership with national agricultural institutes for vulnerable coastal zones.

🚜

Farmer Advisory & Field Consultation

Agronomists deployed directly to village clusters offering scientific soil testing, fertilizer scheduling, and integrated pest management (IPM).

🔬

Quality Verification Laboratory

Seed testing laboratories conducting purity evaluations, moisture testing, seed-borne pathogen screening, and germination validation.

🤝

Co-Operative Seed Multipliers

Contract farming agreements with certified progressive seed growers ensuring transparent buyback pricing and steady rural livelihoods.
