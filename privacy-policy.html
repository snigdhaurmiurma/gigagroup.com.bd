1. Scope & Corporate Commitment

Giga Group of Bangladesh ("Giga Group", "we", "our", or "us") operates as a multifaceted commercial conglomerate orchestrating ten operating companies across enterprise software, creative marketing, agricultural sovereignty, commodities, logistics, and social foundation.

This Privacy Policy outlines our principles regarding the gathering, utilization, storage, and safeguarding of personal and commercial data collected through our corporate portal (gigagroup.com.bd), digital applications, client inquiry systems, and enterprise portals. We comply strictly with the laws of the People's Republic of Bangladesh, including the Information and Communication Technology (ICT) Act, the Digital Security Act, and international data protection standards.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect data necessary to provide high-quality enterprise solutions, business partnerships, customer service, and regulatory compliance:

A. Voluntary Business Inquiries

Names, official email addresses, corporate telephone numbers, designation, and enterprise project scopes submitted through our RFQ, partnership, or contact forms.

B. Technical & Operational Metadata

Browser specifications, IP addresses, access timestamps, referral sources, and page interaction metrics collected to optimize platform speed and prevent unauthorized intrusions.

3. How Information Is Used

  • Enterprise Service Delivery: Providing custom cloud ERP configurations, digital campaign executions, hardware servicing, or cold-chain logistics routing.
  • Client Communications: Responding to tenders, requests for proposals (RFP), technical support tickets, and corporate milestone announcements.
  • Fraud Prevention & Security: Monitoring network integrity, preventing denial-of-service attempts, and auditing system access logs.
  • Legal Compliance: Adhering to statutory tax, corporate auditing, and legal regulatory disclosure obligations under Bangladesh government directives.

4. Subsidiary Inter-Company Data Handling

Giga Group consists of 10 operating subsidiaries: Giga Soft Limited, Giga Digital Agency, PC Care & Engineering, Tender Barta, Giga Food Limited, Krishi Beez Bank, Giga Shop Limited, Giga Enterprise, Rider Service Limited, and Giga Foundation.

When you initiate a business inquiry intended for a specific concern (e.g. requesting software architecture from Giga Soft or logistics fulfillment via Rider Service), relevant project parameters are shared securely with the respective operating unit. We never sell, lease, or monetize client or partner data to third-party commercial brokers under any circumstance.

5. Security Safeguards & Data Retention

We implement multi-layered institutional safeguards, including 256-bit SSL/TLS end-to-end transport encryption, firewalled server clusters, access-controlled databases, and routine vulnerability audits. Corporate records are retained only for the duration necessary to satisfy contractual, operational, and statutory requirements.

6. Cookie Policy

Our website utilizes essential cookies to preserve your theme preference (Dark Mode or Light Mode) and ensure seamless navigation across our sister concern directory. These functional cookies do not extract private personal information and can be managed directly via your browser preferences.

7. Terms of Use & Intellectual Property

All intellectual property, trademarks, logos, diagrams, case studies, software descriptions, and brand assets displayed on gigagroup.com.bd are the exclusive property of Giga Group of Bangladesh. Unauthorized reproduction, modification, scraping, or distribution of our proprietary content without explicit prior written authorization is strictly prohibited under copyright laws.
