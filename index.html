about us

Giga Group is one of the largest conglomerates in Bangladesh with Giga Limited. Over the started business, the Group has expanded its businesses into other industries including software, ICT, Textile, real estate, construction, energy, plastic, furniture, food, welding and electrodes, electronics, logistics, and digital marketing.

Our mission

The mission of the group is to concentrate the total efforts for growth, expansion and diversify towards the market needs. The continued dedication for providing quality products, outstanding services and competitive prices has been apparent in the ability to maintain and also improve market shares in virtually every business segment the group serves.

Our Vision

Become a well-diversified leader in respective industries, through the efficient utilization of resources, technology, market wisdom, and reputation.
