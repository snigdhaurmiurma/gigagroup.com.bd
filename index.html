Corporate Overview

One of Bangladesh's Most Progressive Conglomerates

Giga Group of Bangladesh is a multifaceted business federation committed to technological empowerment, indigenous manufacturing, and sustainable market distribution.

From our central headquarters at Kawran Bazar, Dhaka, Giga Group coordinates strategic ventures in cutting-edge enterprise software, full-scale digital advertising, agricultural seed banking, food packaging, tech maintenance, and logistics.

Our Mission

To deliver premier technological and industrial products that elevate quality of life, streamline commerce, and contribute directly to Bangladesh's macroeconomic self-reliance.

Our Vision

To stand as the most trusted national conglomerate across software, commerce, and agriculture through unwavering integrity, technological wisdom, and customer-first execution.
