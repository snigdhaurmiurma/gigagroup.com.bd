Products & Capabilities

Enterprise Solutions & Offerings

Comprehensive services and technical solutions engineered by PC Care & Engineering Limited.

🖥️

Enterprise Server Deployments

Procurement, installation, and configuration of high-density rack servers, blade systems, SAN/NAS storage arrays, and redundant backup power.

🔧

Annual Maintenance Contracts (AMC)

Proactive monthly preventative maintenance, 2-hour critical breakdown response, loaner hardware replacement, and guaranteed zero downtime SLAs.

🌐

Structured Cabling & Networking

Cat6/Cat6A fiber optic network deployment, server room rack organization, enterprise switching, and corporate Wi-Fi mesh systems.

🛡️

Cybersecurity & Firewall Setup

Configuration of Fortinet, Sophos, and Cisco hardware firewalls, VPN tunneling for branch offices, and intruder prevention systems.

⚡

Component & Chip-Level Repair

Specialized lab diagnostics for high-end server motherboards, industrial power supplies, medical hardware boards, and precision electronics.

💾

Disaster Recovery & Data Storage

Automated off-site backup synchronisation, RAID data recovery, tape archive systems, and ransomware-proof immutable storage setups.
