Products & Capabilities

Enterprise Solutions & Offerings

Comprehensive services and technical solutions engineered by Giga Food Limited.

🌾

Pure Mustard & Edible Oils

Cold-pressed kachi ghani mustard oil, chemical-free sunflower oil, and refined soybean oils retaining natural vitamins and sharp authentic aroma.

🍚

Premium Aromatic & Rice Grains

Directly sourced Chinigura, Nazirshail, and Miniket rice varieties polished without toxic whitening chemicals and packaged in moisture-proof sacks.

🌶️

Pure Ground Spices

Turmeric, chili, coriander, and cumin powdered from graded whole crops, rigorously lab-tested to ensure zero lead or synthetic colouring.

🍯

Natural Honey & Organic Preserves

Raw unprocessed Sundarban and mustard flower honey, handmade fruit preserves, and natural ghee prepared according to traditional recipes.

📦

Hygienic Vacuum Packaging

Automated nitrogen-flushed food-grade packaging preventing oxidation, rancidity, and insect infestation without artificial preservatives.

🚚

Nationwide FMCG Supply Chain

Integrated distribution network supplying superstores, regional wholesalers, and neighborhood grocery stores across all eight divisions.
