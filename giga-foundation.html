Products & Capabilities

Enterprise Solutions & Offerings

Comprehensive services and technical solutions engineered by Giga Foundation.

🎓

Merit-Based Youth Scholarships

Financial stipends covering college and university tuition for brilliant students from economically distressed families across rural districts.

🌊

Rapid Emergency Disaster Relief

Dedicated volunteer emergency teams deploying clean drinking water, dry rations, hygiene supplies, and emergency shelter kits during floods and cyclones.

🏥

Free Healthcare & Eye Clinics

Mobile medical camps providing free doctor consultations, basic medicines, diagnostic screenings, and cataract surgery camps in remote villages.

💻

Digital Literacy & Youth Coding Camps

Establishing community computer centers and conducting free foundational software, graphic design, and internet literacy courses for rural youth.

🌳

Afforestation & Environmental Stewardship

Annual tree plantation drives across riverbank embankments to mitigate soil erosion, sequester carbon, and restore ecological equilibrium.

👩‍🌾

Women Micro-Entrepreneurship Grants

Small seed capital grants and sewing equipment donations enabling rural women to launch home-based textile and handicraft micro-businesses.
